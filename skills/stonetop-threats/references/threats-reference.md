# Stonetop threat types — full reference

The eight threat types, each with its nature, its GM moves, and a few worked examples with instincts. Use this when SKILL.md's summary table isn't enough — e.g. picking a type for an ambiguous threat, or hunting for move inspiration when writing grim portents.

If a type still isn't obvious after skimming its moves, ask: does it act mostly through emotion/circumstance with no real leader (rabble), through formal power structures (institution), or is it really just one dangerous person (villain)? That split resolves most ambiguous cases.

## Affliction

Not a person, monster, or thing — a behavior, circumstance, or condition that makes people suffer, fight, or fret. Can affect one PC, a group, a population, or a whole region.

GM moves: worsen or quicken · spread to others/suck others in · mutate, take on a new form/aspect · eat away at something/someone · strip someone of honor/dignity · drive someone to desperation · justify selfishness, neglect · drive a wedge between people · cause delusion, stubbornness, foolishness · sow panic or despair · trigger shortages, hoarding · prompt violence, hatred, blame

Examples: the black pox (*to infect and spread*) · a feud between two NPCs (*to make people pick sides*) · a drought (*to deplete resources*) · a magical taint (*to cause delusions*) · an entrenched prejudice (*to place limitations, on everyone*) · resentment toward an outside group (*to prevent cooperation*) · the slave trade (*to deny humanity*)

## Beast

Monsters, animals, or NPCs driven by basic need and instinct — even smart ones just follow appetite, lust, or ego rather than plans. Can be a single creature, a family/pack/herd, or a whole species.

GM moves: show up where it's not wanted · stalk or pursue prey · protect its home or family · make a show of strength, aggression · build or expand a nest/den/lair · modify its environment · flee or panic or rage · consume something (or someone) · grow or diminish, in size or numbers

Examples: cave bears (*to fill their bellies, protect their young*) · a protected herd (*to act with impunity*) · a boastful strongman NPC (*to make sure everyone knows how great he is*) · a territorial predator (*to dominate its territory*) · a breeding swarm (*to breed and spread out*) · a compulsive environment-shaper (*to compulsively shape his environment*)

## Institution

People, groups, or positions with power in a community (or several) — formal (a cult, a government) or informal (a council of elders). Often has a leader, but the instinct belongs to the institution as a whole, and it acts through individuals invested in its continuing influence.

GM moves: sway public opinion · put someone in their place · change a rule, law, or custom · acquire leverage, resources, influence · denounce something or someone · support a course of action · recruit new members or minions · squabble amongst themselves · change leadership · negotiate a deal or treaty · send someone else to do their dirty work

Examples: a priesthood (*to preserve civilization, at all costs*) · a town council (*to maintain the status quo*) · a knowledge-hoarding cult (*to hoard knowledge, power, people*) · a labor union (*to undermine the bosses' control*)

## MacGuffin

A thing — object, place, or secret — that stirs up conflict, either because it's inherently dangerous or because people fight over it. Instinct should describe *how it causes trouble*, not what it does: a weather-controlling artifact is a threat because others covet it, not because of the weather.

GM moves: reveal a secret · draw attention to itself · point to something else · generate envy/fear/discord · weigh heavily, become a burden · be the target of theft · go missing · perform its function, heedlessly · fail at the worst possible moment · leave its mark on someone or thing · become something greater, or lesser

Examples: a vault of dangerous artifacts (*to harbor badness*) · a tree of secrets (*to reveal dangerous secrets*) · a cursed weapon (*to encourage violence*) · a coveted artifact (*to inspire jealousy*)

## Magical entity

Spirits, Fae, gods or their agents, things from below — beings for whom mundane needs (food, shelter) are alien or irrelevant. Often bound by strange, arbitrary rules and forced to act subtly or through intermediaries.

GM moves: spy on someone, unseen/from afar · sense powerful longings/emotions · appear in glimpses, dreams, visions · offer service, secrets, power · demand an oath or sacrifice · lay a curse · twist a bargain to its favor · send forth minions to do its bidding · shape its environs, per its nature · pursue alien goals · foster rivalries with similar powers · grow or diminish in strength

Examples: a serpent god (*to smother life, hope, and light*) · a spider mother (*to see her many children spread*) · a bound idol-spirit (*to break free of its bondage*) · a vain Fae (*to be adored*) · a hedonistic ghost (*to enjoy the pleasures of the flesh*) · a mummified ruler at peace (*to preserve its glorious afterlife*)

## Rabble

A group, with or without a leader, united by emotion, blood, or circumstance rather than shared purpose. Any given member might not share — or might actively oppose — the group's instinct.

GM moves: grow or gather in numbers · claim territory or resources · fall under a (new) leader's sway · undergo internal turmoil · make a show of strength/numbers · declare an enemy or an alliance · turn on one of their own · overwhelm a position or a weaker group · despoil, loot, pillage, burn · refuse to be controlled/contained · disperse, scatter, flee

Examples: refugees (*to not fit in*) · a slave rebellion (*to throw off their shackles*) · bandits (*to do whatever the hell they want*) · Fae revelers (*to drag others into their wanton excess*) · a close-knit family (*to close ranks and protect their own*) · a secret cult (*to pursue power, fortune, and respect*)

## Villain

An individual with the ruthlessness, drive, and power to make life awful for others. Acts through minions when possible; when acting directly, does so from strength or safety, and usually has a backup plan.

GM moves: grasp power, recklessly · gain followers or allies · find someone's weakness · make an offer, with strings attached · demand concessions, obedience, or respect · make threats, veiled or not · outmaneuver their enemies · attack cautiously, holding reserves · attack ruthlessly, with little warning · reveal preparations made in advance · sacrifice another to advance a goal · betray an ally or a trust · take a prisoner · do the unthinkable

Examples: a power-hungry lord (*to maintain or increase his power*) · a slaver (*to glorify himself and his clan*) · a schmuck-turned-sorcerer (*to get what he thinks the world owes him*) · a cult leader (*to set free the Living Darkness*) · a general chasing past glory (*to return his people to glory*)

## Wildcard

Someone who causes trouble by being a mystery, by being cared-about-but-untrustworthy, or by being opposed to the PCs without being a villain. The catch-all when nothing else fits.

Build triangles: connect the wildcard to a PC (old friendship, unresolved history) and to a second party, then let the wildcard's instinct create friction between them.

GM moves: aggressively pursue their instinct · show their worth, or lack thereof · display the contents of their heart · provide advice/aid, wanted or not · reveal a secret, or keep one closely · draw attention to themselves/others · appear unannounced · act strangely (for them) · bear witness · tell stories (true or not) · make/keep/break/demand a promise · force an issue or a confrontation · stand resolute and refuse to budge

Examples: a busybody (*to cling to how things "should" be*) · an impulsive initiate (*to act impulsively*) · someone trying to impress others (*to try to impress [specific people]*) · a heartbreaker (*to toy with hearts*)

## Fully worked example: Sajra the Swyn

This is the source book's complete walkthrough of writing a threat end to end, kept here as a model to imitate.

> **SAJRA THE SWYN (villain)**
> **INSTINCT** *to indulge its ego*
>
> Recently moved into a ruined Green Lord's tomb about 1½ days from town (from where?). Has dominated a few nests of crinwin (rabble; to covet); they've been stealing children for it. Has Wynfor (Caradoc's nephew) and another child (who?).
>
> - More children go missing
> - Some of the (entranced) children return
> - Children recruit followers
> - Reveals itself, demands servitude
> - **IMPENDING DOOM:** Subjugates the town, killing or mesmerizing any opposition
>
> **STAKES:** Will Wynfor's parents forgive Caradoc? What will become of the stolen children brought under Sajra's control?
>
> **GM MOVES**
> - Plant a suggestion in another's mind
> - Turn the weak-willed into fawning servants

Note how the grim portents were built backward from the doom: villain moves *demand concessions, obedience, or respect* → the ultimatum; *gain followers or allies* → recruiting via returned children; the swyn's own monster move (turning victims into servants) supplied the mechanism. Each step is a concrete, observable escalation, not a task for the PCs to complete.

## More example threats

> **TEGWEN (wildcard)** — *to undermine others' worth*. Blodwen's mother, jealous of her importance to the village. Resents Rhianna, distrusts Vahid, thinks Caradoc a fool. **Stakes:** How far will she go to control Blodwen? How much will Blodwen put up with? **GM moves:** provide (unwanted?) advice or aid · force an issue or a confrontation · wield guilt like a weapon

> **GARET'S WOUND (affliction)** — *to keep Garet in danger*. A leg wound that won't heal right. Portents: wound isn't healing, weak and slow → gets infected, needs amputation → feverish, delirious → **doom:** Garet dies. **Stakes:** What decisions will Rhianna make? How will Blodwen handle it?

> **MUTRA THE TEETH (villain)** — *to acquire*. A mining/salvage boss who wants more Green Lord artifacts. Portents: pays scouts to case Stonetop → embeds an agent → agent identifies key treasures → demands them for a pittance, or else → **doom:** agents kill someone, steal the treasures. **GM moves:** interrupt trade with Gordin's Delve · turn old allies against Stonetop

> **CRINWIN (rabble)** — *to covet humanity*, freed of Sajra's control. Portents: reestablish nests → attacks on travelers → nighttime raids on the village → bigger nests, anyone in the Wood is attacked → **doom:** full invasion. Custom rule: when the village deploys its militia against crinwin raids, add "the crinwin steal 1d4 Surplus" and "an important item goes missing" to the 7-9 options.

> **THE PALE CRINWIN (wildcard)** — *to learn more and more*. A curious crinwin, fascinated by writing and drawing. **Stakes:** Will the PCs learn to communicate with it? Will villagers ever see crinwin as more than vermin? Custom player move: communicating with it via pantomime rolls +INT (10+ message received, 7-9 gist only, 6- badly garbled).

> **NAILADD SWARM (beast)** — *to breed and spread out*, giant spiders spreading via waystones at the Mother-of-Spiders' behest. Portents: nearest waystone infested → game scarce, hunters attacked → spiders raid the village → **doom:** overrun and infest the village. **Stakes:** Will Blodwen bring herself to help kill them? Will Andras overcome his fear of spiders?

> **FARMERS GUILD (rabble)** — *to improve their lot in life*. Idealists wanting a council seat. Portents: council refuses to recognize them → guild strikes → strike broken violently → resentment grows, loyalists defect → **doom:** riots and fire. **Stakes:** Will Dermos and Brin get together? Will Kiran follow orders or conscience?

> **THE MARSHEDGE COUNCIL (institution)** — *to preserve the status quo*. Guild heads and Old Family reps. **GM moves:** cut off Stonetop's supply of something · stall, bicker, refuse to make decisions

> **THE WHITE HAND (institution)** — *to contain the evil of the lakes, no matter what it takes*. A Hillfolk sect patrolling the Steplands. **Stakes:** How will the PCs react if they learn its purpose? **GM moves:** denounce trespassers as unclean · sense the taint of corruption · spread word among the Hillfolk · outmaneuver their prey

> **THE MINDGEM'S HEART (MacGuffin)** — *to empower, ignite, awaken*. A burning shard of makerglass kept in a PC's hearth. **Stakes:** What will the owner do with it? How will others react to its presence? **GM moves:** perform its function, heedlessly · draw attention to itself · catch something on fire · provide a potent supply of magical energy

> **TRADE EMBARGO (affliction)** — *to punish Stonetop*, imposed after the PCs backed the Farmers Guild. Custom rules instead of portents: Outfit starts with 1 fewer small item and supplies have 1 fewer use; Recover heals 1 HP less using Stonetop supplies; Trade or Barter through Marshedge has disadvantage. This shows an affliction can be defined entirely by custom rule-changes rather than portents.

> **THE SHADOWY PRESENCE (magical entity)** — *to devour life, light, warmth*. A bound Green Lord's wraith, awakened by the Mindgem's Heart. Portents: an earthquake frees it → rampages through the Wood, feeding → catches the Heart's scent, moves toward the village → attacks villagers at night → **doom:** devours the Heart, becomes a permanent vortex of death and darkness. **Stakes:** How will its link to Caradoc affect what happens?
